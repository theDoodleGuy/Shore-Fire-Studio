Fairquestions.

Do I need to be good with tech?

No. If you can send a photo on WhatsApp, you can update your site. We'll show you how and stick around after launch.

How long does a site take?

Most go live in two to four weeks, depending on how quickly we get your photos and words together.

Will AI write my whole website?

No. AI helps us work faster, but every site is planned, designed and checked by a person who knows your area and your trade.

What does AI mentoring cover?

Whatever eats your time. Usually quotes, emails, social posts, job write-ups and admin. We pick real tasks and get them done together.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, your content, your site. If you ever move on, it goes with you.
